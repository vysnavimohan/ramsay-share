# Ramsay Demo — Run of Show

**The story:** an ops manager runs 5 hospitals. She can see the numbers. She just can't
ask questions across them easily. We start with what she has today, then show the app that
answers anything, then show where deeper modelling takes it.

Three acts. ~15 minutes.

---

## Act 1 — The dashboard (where she is today)

Open **"Ramsay Health — Group Operations (5 Hospitals)"**.

This is the ops manager's morning view. Theatre utilisation, admissions, bed occupancy,
diagnostic slots, waiting lists — all five hospitals on one page.

Point out:
- She can see Springfield is running hot and Oaklands has room.
- The high-level picture is right there. No analyst needed.

Then hit the limit:
- The dashboard tells her **what**, not **why**.
- The moment she has a real question — "which site has the worst waits, and what's
  driving it?" — she's stuck. She clicks between tiles. Or she files a request and waits.
- Every new question is a new chart or a new ticket.

**Line to land:** "This is great for a fixed set of questions. It falls apart the moment
you have a new one."

---

## Act 2 — The AI supervisor app (one place to ask anything)

Open the **AI Supervisor app**.

This is the one-stop shop. She types the question in plain English. No dashboards to
navigate, no ticket to raise.

Ask one of the seed questions, e.g.:
> "Which hospital has the lowest wait times? Compare to the ones with the highest and tell
> me the root cause."

Show what happens:
- The app breaks the question into parts.
- It routes each part to the right specialist — capacity, patient flow, finance, workforce.
- It pulls the answers back and gives her one reply.
- The trace panel shows the working, so she can trust it.

Ask a second, different one to prove the range:
> "How many patients over 55 referred for Trauma and Orthopaedics are on an admitted RTT
> pathway, by hospital?"

**Line to land:** "One place. Any question. Across every topic — capacity, finance,
workforce, waiting lists. This is what the dashboard can't be."

---

## Act 3 — Switch Capacity (where modelling takes it further)

Open the **Switch Capacity** tab.

Q&A is powerful. But some questions need real modelling, not just a lookup. This is the
example.

Walk through the scenario:
> "Close 2 theatres for 3 days at Springfield, 14–16 Sep. What's the impact? Which
> surgeries are hit? Can they move? When's the best time to do it with least disruption?"

Show the model working it out:
- Which cases are affected.
- Where they can be shifted.
- The knock-on to waits and capacity.
- The best date to do it.

**Line to land:** "This is the next step. Not just answering questions — modelling
decisions before you make them. Same data, same platform."

---

## Wrap
